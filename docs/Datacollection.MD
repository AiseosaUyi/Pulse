## User Interviews (Planned)

### Interview Script
**Target:** Founders/marketers managing 2+ brands simultaneously

1. Walk me through how you managed marketing across your brands yesterday. Step by step.
2. Which platforms are you active on? How do you decide what to post where?
3. How do you track what's working? Show me your spreadsheet/notes/system.
4. What's the most annoying part of managing multiple brands' marketing?
5. Have you tried any tools? What worked? What didn't? Why did you stop?
6. If I could eliminate ONE thing from your weekly marketing routine, what would it be?
7. How much time do you spend per week on marketing across all brands?
8. Would you pay $X/month to cut that time in half? (test multiple price points)

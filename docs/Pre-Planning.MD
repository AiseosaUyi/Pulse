# Pre-Planning: PULSE Marketing OS

## Phase 1: Problem Definition

### Problem Statement

Small multi-brand founders (running 2-5 startups simultaneously) are managing marketing across 4+ social platforms per brand with zero unified system. They tab-switch between Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, manually track performance in spreadsheets, and have no data-driven view of what's working, what needs attention, or where to spend the next dollar.

### Why Now

1. **AI cost collapse (2025-2026):** LLM APIs dropped 10-50x in 18 months. AI-generated content, automated insights, and intelligent scheduling are now viable for tools charging $50-200/mo, not $2,000/mo enterprise contracts.
2. **Creator-founder explosion:** The solo founder running multiple brands is the new normal. YC's "20x company" thesis (10 people + AI = billion-dollar company) means marketing teams are shrinking while the number of brands per founder is growing.
3. **Platform fragmentation:** TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn's creator push, X's algorithm shifts. Managing 4+ platforms per brand is now table stakes, not optional. The coordination cost scales multiplicatively with each new brand.
4. **Existing tools don't do multi-tenant:** Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout Social, Later. All single-brand. Switching between "workspaces" is clunky. None of them think in terms of a founder running a portfolio of brands from one command center.

### Who Is Affected

- **Primary:** Solo/small-team founders running 2+ brands with active social media presence
- **Secondary:** Small marketing agencies managing 5-15 client brands
- **Tertiary:** Creator-entrepreneurs with personal brand + product brands

### Evidence Gaps (Honest Assessment)

- No external user validation yet. Founder IS the user, which is the strongest v0 signal but also the biggest bias risk.
- No pricing validation. No one has been asked to pay.
- No usage data. The product is 100% mock data, 3 git commits, zero real platform API connections.
- "Multi-tenant marketing OS" sounds big. The question is whether the multi-tenant angle is a real wedge or a nice-to-have feature on top of existing tools.

### Assumptions to Validate

1. Multi-brand founders actually want ONE tool (vs. one tool per brand)
2. The pain of tab-switching is severe enough to pay $X/mo to eliminate
3. AI-generated content suggestions are trusted enough to act on
4. Social platform APIs are stable enough to build a reliable product on
5. The market is big enough (multi-brand founders) to reach $100M valuation

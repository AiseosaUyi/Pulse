## Vision

### 10x Check
PULSE doesn't just watch competitors — it becomes Abas's strategic brain. Cross-brand intelligence spots patterns across the Gruve/Sippy portfolio. Trend prediction gets ahead of competitors. A learning loop calibrates recommendations to what actually works for YOUR brands. "Ask Pulse" turns the feed into a strategic conversation partner. The system goes from "here's what competitors did" to "here's what you should do, and here's why, based on everything we've learned about your market."

### Platonic Ideal
Abas opens PULSE at 8am. Within 30 seconds, 3 bullets tell him what happened overnight. He sees a competitor's viral post and taps "Steal this" — a content brief appears, tailored to Gruve's voice and Lagos audience. He asks Pulse "should we be on Threads?" and gets a data-backed answer grounded in competitor performance data. Over time, PULSE's recommendations sharpen because it learns from what the team actually publishes. The weekly digest is so good it replaces the Monday strategy meeting. Cross-brand insights emerge naturally: "Rooftop event content outperforms indoor content 3x for both Gruve and Sippy." Abas goes from "flying blind" to "I have an unfair advantage."

## Key Decisions from Review

1. **Approach B confirmed** with Week 2 hard validation gate. The premise test revealed competitor monitoring is ad hoc, not systematic — the feed creates a new behavior.
2. **Route group rename** `(discovery)` → `(intelligence)` is safe (parenthesized folders don't affect URLs in App Router).
3. **Morning briefing is the habit trigger** for the Week 2 adoption gate. Without it, the feed is passive.
4. **Cross-brand queries need tenant-crossing access** — this conflicts with RLS. Requires a service-role query or cross-tenant materialized view.
5. **Smart notifications start as email** (Phase 2 MVP). Browser push deferred due to permission grant UX complexity.
6. **Learning loop needs 30+ data points** to be useful. Manual "I published this" checkbox in Phase 1, auto-detection in Phase 2+.
